How the software works today

These are facts about the current software, written down so that the final policy can be checked against them.

  • Same-device and computer games run entirely in your browser. Nothing about those games is sent anywhere or stored.
  • Online games and private rooms use a game server. It keeps in memory (not on disk) the display name you type, a random seat token, and when you last contacted it, for as long as the room exists. Rooms are deleted shortly after a game ends or both players leave, and an unjoined room expires after 30 minutes.
  • Your browser keeps the seat token in its local storage so that a reload can rejoin your seat. It is removed when you leave the room.
  • Network addresses are held in memory for about a minute to limit abuse.
  • The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts.

What the final policy must cover

  • What data the service collects (for example usernames, connection data and logs) and why.
  • How private rooms are created, how long they exist and when they're deleted.
  • Analytics, cookies and similar storage, and whether consent is needed.
  • Third-party services such as hosting, content delivery and analytics.
  • How long data is kept, who can access it, and how people can exercise their rights.
  • How to contact us about privacy, and the date the policy took effect.
