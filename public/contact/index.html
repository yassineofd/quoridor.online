Contact Us

Send us your feedback, questions or a problem report.

Questions, ideas, or something not working? We read every message. Tell us what you think of the game, report a problem, or suggest what we should add next.

You can write to us at lahssanyassine1@gmail.com, or use the form below to start a message in your email app.

Please don't include passwords or other sensitive personal data.

Reporting a problem

The more detail you give, the faster we can find the cause. Please include:

  • The page or game mode you were using (for example online play, against the computer, or one device).
  • Your device, operating system, and browser with its version.
  • What you did, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. For an online game, the time it happened.

Your information

We use your message and email address only to answer you. The form doesn't send anything by itself: it opens a draft in your own email app, and nothing is sent until you send it. See the Privacy Policy for how the site handles information.
