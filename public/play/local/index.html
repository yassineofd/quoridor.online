Play Quoridor with a friend

Share one screen and take turns on the same board.

Two players, one device

You share the screen and take turns on the same board. The active player is always named on screen, with a clear cue when the turn changes.

  • Player 1 uses the filled cyan pawn and moves first, from the bottom edge.
  • Player 2 uses the white ring pawn, from the top edge.
  • Pass the device after each move.
