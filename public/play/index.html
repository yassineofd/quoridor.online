How to Play Quoridor

Quoridor is a strategy board game built around two choices: move your pawn toward the goal or place a wall to slow your opponent down. Every wall uses a limited resource, so the best move is not always the most obvious one.

Your objective is to reach the opposite side of the board before your opponent. You can make progress along your own route, create detours for your opponent, and adapt your strategy as the board changes. However, you must always leave each player a legal path to their goal.

Improve Your Quoridor Strategy

Winning takes more than moving forward. Think ahead before placing a wall, compare possible routes, and decide when to advance your pawn instead of using a barrier. Understanding how your decisions affect both players can help you make better moves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play Quoridor online for free?

Yes. You can play Quoridor online on this website without registration. Choose an available game mode and start playing.

Can I play Quoridor against a computer?

Yes. Select the computer mode and choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert difficulty.

Can I play Quoridor with a friend?

You can play locally on the same device or create a private online room and invite your friend.

Do I need an account to play Quoridor?

No account is required to start playing. Online play uses a username rather than a registration process.

What is the objective of Quoridor?

The goal is to move your pawn to the opposite side of the board before your opponent. You can also place walls to create detours, but you cannot completely block a player's route to the goal.
