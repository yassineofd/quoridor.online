How to Play Quoridor

The objective is simple: move your pawn to the opposite side of the board before your opponent does. Walls are your tool for making their trip longer than yours.

  1. Race to the other sideYour pawn starts in the middle of your edge and must reach any square on the far edge.
  2. Move or place a wallOn each turn you either step your pawn or place one wall.
  3. Never seal the pathA wall is only legal if both players still have a legal path to their goal.

Why the walls matter

A good wall costs you one turn but costs your opponent several. Judging that trade, again and again, is the whole game.

A Strategy Board Game of Movement and Tactics

There are no dice, cards or hidden information. Every game is decided by what you plan and what you notice.

  • Route planningCount the shortest path for both pawns before every move.
  • BlockingPlace walls that force long detours, not walls that add a single step.
  • TimingYou only have 10 walls, so choose the moment each one pays off most.
  • Anticipating your opponentThink about where their pawn is heading and which walls they could play next.

Frequently Asked Questions About Quoridor

Can I play Quoridor online for free?

Yes. Playing is free and you don't need to register. You can play online against another person, against the computer or with a friend on the same device. Online games pair you with another player through matchmaking, or you can invite a friend to a private room.

Can I play Quoridor against a computer?

Yes. Choose play against the computer, then pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. The computer opponent runs in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere.

Can two people play on the same device?

Yes. In local play two players share one screen and take turns on the same board. Player 1 moves first.

Do I need an account?

No. Nothing on the site requires an account. When you play online you just choose a display name, which is not proof of identity.

How many walls does each player get?

In a two-player game each player starts with 10 walls. Once they are used up you can only move your pawn. The Quoridor rules cover wall placement in full.
