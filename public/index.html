How the game works

Quoridor is easy to learn and surprisingly deep. You win by reaching the opposite side of the board, and walls are your tool for making your opponent's trip longer than yours.

  1. Race to the other sideYour pawn starts at the middle of your edge and must reach any square on the far edge.
  2. Move or blockOn each turn you either step your pawn one square or place a wall.
  3. Never seal the pathWalls can lengthen a route but never close it completely.

Why the walls matter

A good wall costs you one turn but costs your opponent several. Judging that trade, again and again, is the whole game.

Frequently asked questions

What is the goal of Quoridor?

Move your pawn to any square on the opposite side of the board before your opponent reaches yours. Each turn you either move your pawn or place a wall to slow your opponent down.

How many players are there?

The standard game is for two players. The physical game also has a four-player version, but every mode planned for this site is a two-player game.

Can I block my opponent completely?

No. A wall is only legal if both players still have at least one open route to their goal side. See the Quoridor rules for the details.

Which ways to play are there?

You can play online against another person, against the computer or with a friend on the same device.

Do I need an account?

No. Nothing on the site requires an account. Where a mode needs a name, you pick a display name; it is not proof of identity.

How many walls does each player get?

In a two-player game each player starts with 10 walls. Once they are used up, you can only move your pawn.
