How online games work today

These are facts about the current software, written down so that the final terms can be checked against them.

  • If a player's connection is lost for more than a minute during an online game, that player forfeits and the opponent wins.
  • Leaving or resigning mid-game counts as a forfeit. Either player may ask for a rematch after a game; the first move then passes to the other player.
  • An invitation (link and pairing code) expires after 30 minutes if nobody joins. Rooms and their state are not kept after play ends.
  • Usernames are display names, not accounts. Two players may choose the same name.

What the final terms must cover
