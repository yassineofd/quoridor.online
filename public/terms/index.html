4. Multiplayer behavior and room sharing

  • Display names. A name is 2 to 20 characters and is not an account, so it gives you no ownership of the name and two players may choose the same one. Choose a name you would be comfortable having shown to a stranger. Do not use a name that is offensive, hateful, sexually explicit or threatening, that impersonates another person or the site, or that reveals personal details such as your real name or email address.
  • Conduct toward other players. Play fairly and respectfully. Do not harass others, and do not abandon games repeatedly to frustrate opponents.
  • Shared links and codes act like keys. A private room is reached through a long random link and a short pairing code. Anyone who has the link or code while a seat is open can take that seat. You are responsible for sharing it only with the person you want to play, and for any use made of a link or code you share publicly.
  • Invitations expire. An invitation expires after 30 minutes if nobody joins. Rooms and their state are not kept after play ends.
  • Disconnection and forfeit. If a player's connection is lost for more than a minute during an online game, that player forfeits and the opponent wins. Leaving or resigning mid-game also counts as a forfeit. A player who does not move within the time limit has a move played for them by the server. After a game, either player may ask for a rematch, and the first move then passes to the other player.
