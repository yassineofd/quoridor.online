About us

An accessible, browser-based place for strategy gaming.

Our purpose

Quoridor Online is a simple, accessible place to play and learn the Quoridor strategy game in your browser, with no account needed.

What we focus on

  • A clear, distraction-free board that works on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Easy ways to start: online, against the computer, or with a friend on one device.
  • Honest, useful guides on how to play Quoridor, the rules and strategy.

Where things stand

You can play Quoridor in the browser. We only describe a feature as available once it works.

Independence

Quoridor Online is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the designer or publisher of the Quoridor board game.
