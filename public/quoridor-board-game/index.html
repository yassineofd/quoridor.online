What the Quoridor board game is

Quoridor is an abstract strategy board game for two or four players. There are no dice, cards or hidden information: both players see everything, so the result depends on planning. It was designed by Mirko Marchesi and published by Gigamic.

Quoridor Online is an independent website. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's designer or publisher.

Board layout and components

A standard set contains a 9×9 board with grooves between the squares, four pawns and twenty walls. In a two-player game each player uses one pawn and ten walls. Pawns start at the middle of opposite edges.

For the exact step-by-step rules, see the Quoridor rules.

The central strategic challenge

The game is a race with a twist. Moving gets you closer to winning, but walls change how far away the finish is for both players. Every turn is a trade-off between making progress and making your opponent's progress harder, and you only have a limited number of walls to spend.

How barriers shape movement

Walls don't capture or remove anything. They only change the shape of the board, turning open squares into corridors and detours. Because a wall can never fully seal a path, a good barrier is one that makes the opponent's shortest route much longer than yours.
